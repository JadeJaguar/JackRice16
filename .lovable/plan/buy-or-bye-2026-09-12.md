# Buy or Bye

## Goal

Build a secure personal finance app that combines monthly budgeting, spending progress, a spreadsheet-style tracker, and an AI purchase consultant. Purchase Pal will use separate, account-saved conversations and can consider the user’s budget and recorded spending.

## Experience

- **Welcome and account access:** A polished sage, sky-blue, and white sign-in screen with curved rectangular fields, a clear “Are you new here? Sign up” switch, email/password access, and using Google sign-in integration only. 
- **Overview:** A rewarding monthly snapshot with planned spending, recorded spending, remaining budget, percentage saved, and progress by category.
- **Budget track:** A guided monthly budget setup with common category buttons. Selecting a category expands its description and examples, then lets the user set or revise the amount. Totals update immediately. Recommends the user to go into the monthly budget to create any adjustments as needed at the bottom of the budget tracker through a hyperlink + arrow to get to the monthly budget seamlessly. 
- **Purchase Pal:** A thread list, new conversation action, dedicated URL for each conversation, photo attachment, product description, streamed answers, visible thinking status, and markdown responses. The assistant considers the selected category’s budget and past spending before guiding the decision. Stores each conversation in a left side panel that can and allows the user to scroll through/search keywords from conversations had before. There can also be the ability to save an item for later by allowing the user to click a heart button to add it to another expandable side panel on the right of the screent storing what items the user hearted. 
- **Tracker:** An editable, spreadsheet-like transaction table plus an AI-assisted logging flow that asks for item, amount, date, and category, then records the expense after confirmation for the user. 
- **Progress:** Clear milestones and positive feedback without shaming, while keeping currency totals and category progress easy to scan. 

## Visual direction

- Use a soft sage, clear sky blue, and clean white palette with dark forest/ink text and accessible contrast. Can have fades/ dabs of color
- Use rounded, readable typography and curved rectangular controls.
- Give track and category controls a solid resting surface with a restrained glass-like hover treatment.
- Keep the layout calm and spacious, with lightweight motion and strong mobile behavior.

## Data and security

- Store each user’s profile, monthly budget plans, category allocations, transactions, Purchase Pal threads, and messages in Lovable Cloud.
- Keep purchase photos in private storage with account-scoped access.
- Apply ownership rules to every record so users can only access their own financial data, chats, and files.
- Derive overview totals and saved percentages from the active month’s budgets and transactions.

## Technical details

- Add authenticated page structure for overview, budget setup, tracker, and `/purchase-pal/:threadId`; protect both pages and server operations.
- Use the generated Cloud client for authentication and account-owned data, with email/password and managed Google sign-in.
- Use AI SDK streaming with `openai/gpt-6-astra`, full conversation history, visible reasoning summaries, and server-side tools for reading budget context and creating confirmed transactions.
- Use AI Elements for the conversation, messages, markdown, prompt input, attachments, thinking state, and tool activity.
- Add explicit handling for invalid requests, missing configuration, credit limits, policy blocks, rate limits, and temporary AI failures.
- Add route-specific titles and social descriptions, and verify desktop and mobile layouts plus sign-in, budgeting, thread restoration, photo upload, tracker updates, and live AI responses.

3. Build overview, monthly budget setup, progress, and tracker workflows.
4. Build persisted Purchase Pal threads and the live AI consultant.
